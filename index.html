Beginner's Guide to Basketball

Learn the basics and enjoy the game!

Inspired by Ulrik Chua's favorite sport

What is Basketball?

Basketball is a sport played by two teams. Each team tries to score points by shooting the ball into the opponent's basket. It is a fun game that needs teamwork, practice, and communication.

Things You Need

Basketball

A ball used to play the game.

Court and Hoop

The area where players play and score.

Sports Shoes

Help players move safely on the court.

Basic Rules

  1. Each team has five players on the court in a standard game.
  2. Players must dribble the ball while moving.
  3. A regular field goal is worth two points, while a shot beyond the three-point line is worth three.
  4. Players should avoid pushing, hitting, and other illegal contact.
  5. The team with the most points at the end wins.

Basic Basketball Skills

Dribbling

Bouncing the ball while moving around the court.

Shooting

Throwing the ball toward the basket to score points.

Passing

Sending the ball to a teammate.

Defense

Trying to stop the opposing team from scoring.

Benefits of Basketball

  • Improves physical fitness.
  • Develops teamwork and communication.
  • Builds discipline and confidence.
  • Helps people make friends.
